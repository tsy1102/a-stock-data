# CODE_OF_CONDUCT

## Community Guidelines

We welcome contributions from everyone. Please follow these simple guidelines when interacting with the project:

- **Be respectful** – Treat everyone with courtesy and kindness.
- **Be constructive** – Provide helpful feedback, and avoid personal attacks.
- **Focus on the project** – Keep discussions relevant to the repository and its goals.
- **No harassment** – Any form of harassment, discrimination, or abusive language is prohibited.
- **Respect privacy** – Do not share personal data of others without consent.

This document is based on the [Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

By participating in this project, you agree to abide by these guidelines.
